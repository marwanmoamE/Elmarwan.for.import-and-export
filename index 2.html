About El Marwan

El Marwan for Import & Export is more than just a trading company — it is a well-established Egyptian enterprise that has grown steadily into one of the region’s most trusted names in automotive spare parts and logistics services. With years of hands-on experience and a forward-thinking mindset, El Marwan is built on a foundation of credibility, operational excellence, and an unrelenting drive to deliver superior value across the entire supply chain.

From humble beginnings rooted in a passion for quality and efficiency, El Marwan has evolved into a multifaceted company that operates at the intersection of international trade and industrial supply. Our headquarters, strategically located in Desouk, Kafr El-Sheikh, Egypt, serves as the nerve center for a nationwide and increasingly global network of clients, partners, and suppliers. This geographical positioning allows us to serve both urban markets and emerging industrial zones with unmatched precision and speed.

At the heart of El Marwan is our commitment to professionalism and long-term relationships. Our journey began with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between international manufacturers and the Egyptian automotive and logistics markets. Over time, this mission expanded as we began to support regional industries with tailored import and export services, warehousing, and distribution solutions. Today, El Marwan doesn’t just buy and sell — we engineer reliable pathways for business continuity and operational success.

Our business philosophy is rooted in four core values: Trust, Quality, Adaptability, and Growth. These values shape our daily operations and strategic decisions. Trust drives our customer relations and transparent service delivery. Quality reflects in every product we handle — we source only certified and tested components, ensuring high performance and durability. Adaptability keeps us ahead in a volatile market, while our hunger for Growth inspires investment in new technology, training, and infrastructure.

El Marwan is structured to operate with efficiency, clarity, and scalability. The company is divided into specialized departments: automotive spare parts sourcing, international shipping coordination, customs and compliance, warehouse management, retail and wholesale distribution, and strategic partnerships. Each department is led by seasoned professionals who bring both industry insight and operational expertise, ensuring all client requirements are met with precision and speed.

Our automotive spare parts division is one of the most extensive in the Egyptian market. We provide OEM and aftermarket parts for a wide range of Japanese, Korean, and European vehicle models, focusing on authenticity and compatibility. With robust stock availability and direct relationships with international manufacturers and exporters, El Marwan is a trusted supplier to both retail customers and large-scale service centers.

Logistics is another cornerstone of our operation. We offer custom solutions for businesses requiring inbound and outbound logistics across Egypt and the MENA region. Whether it’s sea freight, road transport, or consolidated cargo, our logistics team uses advanced tools for tracking, optimization, and delivery management, ensuring that every shipment reaches its destination on time, every time.

We also understand that operational success relies on infrastructure. That’s why we’ve invested heavily in building our warehousing capacity. Our facilities are optimized for both small and bulk storage, temperature-sensitive components, and fast-moving inventory. With secure tracking systems, real-time stock updates, and rigorous audit trails, we ensure that our clients experience zero downtime due to inventory issues.

Beyond our services, El Marwan believes in business ethics and corporate responsibility. We are an inclusive employer, providing training and career development opportunities to local youth. Our operations adhere to both Egyptian regulatory requirements and international trade compliance standards. We prioritize sustainability by optimizing packaging, reducing waste, and embracing energy-efficient logistics practices.

What truly sets El Marwan apart is our leadership mindset. We don’t just follow trends — we aim to set them. Whether it’s through adopting digital transformation tools, launching a customer service chatbot, or developing a client dashboard for live order tracking, our focus remains on delivering a seamless, transparent, and scalable client experience.

In conclusion, “About El Marwan” is not merely a company profile — it’s a declaration of who we are, what we do, and why it matters. We are more than a provider of goods; we are a strategic partner to industries, retailers, workshops, and logistics stakeholders. As we continue to expand locally and globally, El Marwan stands ready to be your trusted companion in trade, logistics, and growth.

Our Services

El Marwan’s services are not simply offerings; they are strategic enablers designed to fuel growth and streamline operations for our clients across Egypt and beyond. Auto Spare Parts

The Auto Spare Parts division at El Marwan is a cornerstone of our business and one of the most sophisticated supply operations in the region. Our catalog includes thousands of parts for Japanese, Korean, German, and American cars, both OEM and high-quality aftermarket. We maintain strong relationships with certified manufacturers across Asia and Europe, allowing us to provide authentic, long-lasting components for various vehicle systems — engines, transmissions, suspensions, brakes, air conditioning,...

With our proprietary inventory management systems, we track product movement in real time. This enables us to maintain optimal stock levels, reduce backorders, and fulfill urgent requests without delay. Our automated reordering system is integrated with sales data, helping us anticipate demand and avoid shortages during peak seasons.

Our technical team also assists clients in choosing the correct part for their vehicle’s make and model, reducing the risk of mismatches. For workshops and B2B clients, we offer consolidated orders, volume-based pricing, and flexible delivery options.

In addition to the products, our packaging and shipping are handled with utmost care. We use reinforced boxing, labeling by QR codes, and climate-safe pallets for sensitive components. It’s not just about the part; it’s about the total delivery experience.

Our ultimate goal is to be the most reliable source of automotive components in the Egyptian market and to support the long-term functionality of every vehicle we help maintain.

Logistics Solutions

Our Logistics Solutions department is designed to solve the end-to-end movement challenges that businesses face every day. We offer road, sea, and multimodal shipping services that connect Egypt with global markets. From containerized shipments to less-than-truckload solutions, we structure transportation to be as efficient and cost-effective as possible.

Our logistics team leverages deep regional knowledge, regulatory familiarity, and real-time analytics to optimize routing, customs handling, and last-mile delivery. Each shipment is monitored using GPS tools, while clients receive proactive updates through our logistics dashboard.

We also handle sensitive cargo and high-value shipments by applying special handling protocols and partnering with insured carriers. For clients in automotive, agriculture, or manufacturing sectors, we provide just-in-time deliveries and vendor-managed inventory solutions that help streamline procurement cycles.

What sets El Marwan apart is our attention to integration. We align logistics planning with warehousing and procurement schedules, ensuring there are no bottlenecks or mismatches across the chain. We also work with freight forwarders, brokers, and port authorities to reduce downtime at borders.

Whether your business ships locally, regionally, or internationally, El Marwan can design a logistics framework that delivers precision, reliability, and cost savings. Logistics is not just movement; it’s execution, intelligence, and partnership.

Warehousing Operations

Warehousing Operations at El Marwan are far more than storage — they are value-driven hubs of efficiency, safety, and accuracy. We manage multiple facilities across key logistics zones in Egypt, with dedicated areas for automotive components, sensitive equipment, and general cargo.

Each warehouse is designed with scalable racking systems, reinforced flooring, and real-time inventory systems that allow clients to access up-to-date information on their stock levels. We use barcode and RFID technologies to enable faster picking, reduced human error, and smoother dispatching.

Our warehousing services include inbound receiving, quality control inspection, itemized stocking, batch tracking, and returns processing. We also offer bonded storage for customs-managed goods and provide temperature-controlled areas for items sensitive to heat or moisture.

Our warehouse staff is trained in both safety procedures and client service protocols, ensuring your products are handled with care and processed with precision. Regular audits and security checks maintain integrity and trust.

In collaboration with our logistics and inventory control teams, the warehousing operation becomes a seamless extension of your business. Whether you need long-term storage, cross-docking services, or rapid fulfillment, El Marwan's warehouses are built to meet today’s demands and tomorrow’s growth.
